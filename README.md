🎬 My YouTube Journey – Vlog
Date: 3rd November 2025

I started this channel on 12th September 2025, posting PUBG emote edits every day 🎮. At first… growth was painfully slow 😔. Only a few dozen subscribers after dozens of videos… I felt frustrated 😢, empty 😞, and sometimes I even thought… maybe I should quit 🥀.

But then… I discovered something that changed everything ⚡. I found channels that focused on one niche — Goku and Saitama edits 🌟. They weren’t just uploading randomly… they were consistent ✔️, focused 🎯, and passionate 🔥. That’s when I realized… success isn’t luck. It’s strategy, creativity 🎨, and persistence 💪.

I decided to pivot my channel… from gaming to entertainment 🌈. I rebranded 🖌️, gave it time ⏳, and waited for the algorithm to recognize my new path 🔄. The first video in this direction got 15k views 👀 and 50 subscribers ✨. My heart raced ❤️. The next video… 65k views 😲! I felt alive 😍. I realized… I could really do this 🌟.

Of course, it wasn’t always smooth. Some videos dropped in views 📉, some subs disappeared mysteriously 👻, doubts crept in 🤔, and there were moments of loneliness 😓. But I never gave up 💥. I reminded myself… this is a marathon 🏃, not a sprint.

Now… I am fully committed to Goku/Saitama edits ⚡. I know the journey won’t always be easy 😅, and there will be days of struggle 😤, but I will keep learning 📚, improving 🛠️, and posting consistently 🏆. Every edit, every video… is a step closer to my dream ✨.

My goal: 50–100k subscribers by the end of 2026 🌍. One day… when I finally see my Silver Play Button 🏅, I will place this vlog next to it and remember every feeling, every struggle, every triumph 💖:

"Yes… you did it 🌟. You believed 🌈, you persevered 💪, and you achieved your dream ✨."

— 3rd November 2025
